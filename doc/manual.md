# InvadeGo-Spatial
Individual based forward simulations of transposable element (TE) invasions in 2D spatial populations.
Uses discrete generations and individuals on a spatial grid. For building the next generation potential mate pairs are picked in the neighborhood based on fitness.
Individuals are hermaphrodites and the sex (male, female) is randomly assigned.
